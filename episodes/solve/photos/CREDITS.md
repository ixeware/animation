# Facts and sources

- C. E. Shannon, "Programming a Computer for Playing Chess", Philosophical Magazine, 1950: game-tree lower bound of about 10^120; a machine of the time would need more than 10^90 years to calculate its first move.
- About 10^80 atoms in the observable universe (common estimate).
- J. Schaeffer et al., "Checkers Is Solved", Science, 19 July 2007: a draw; computers worked on it almost continuously since 1989.
- J. Tromp and P. Österlund: (4.822 ± 0.028) × 10^44 legal chess positions (45 digits).
- Lomonosov 7-piece tablebase, Moscow State University, 2012: about 140 TB; record mate in 549 moves found in 2012.
- 8-piece tablebase: work still underway (Wikipedia, "Endgame tablebase", 2026); longest win found so far 584 moves (DTC, M. Bourzutschky).
