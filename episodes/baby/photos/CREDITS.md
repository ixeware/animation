# Facts and sources

- László Polgár: "geniuses are made, not born"; the experiment began in 1970; his daughters were homeschooled, mainly in chess (Wikipedia, "László Polgár").
- Judit Polgár: grandmaster in December 1991 at 15 years 4 months, breaking Bobby Fischer's record; beat Garry Kasparov in a rapid game in September 2002; has beaten Magnus Carlsen among eleven world champions (Wikipedia, "Judit Polgár").
- Magnus Carlsen: taught by his father at five; grandmaster at 13 (Wikipedia, "Magnus Carlsen").
- Youngest grandmaster ever: Abhimanyu Mishra, 12 years 4 months 25 days, June 2021; still the record as of 2026 (Chess.com).
- The baby in the video is a generic cartoon and does not depict any real child.
