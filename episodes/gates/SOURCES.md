# Bill Gates vs Magnus Carlsen — sources

The game: an exhibition blitz game for the Norwegian-Swedish talk show *Skavlan*, taped in London on 22 or 23 January
2014 (sources differ), broadcast on NRK and SVT on Friday 24 January 2014. Gates had White and 2 minutes, Carlsen had
30 seconds.

`1.e4 Nc6 2.Nf3 d5 3.Bd3 Nf6 4.exd5 Qxd5 5.Nc3 Qh5 6.O-O Bg4 7.h3 Ne5 8.hxg4 Nfxg4 9.Nxe5 Qh2# 0-1`
(chessgames.com, the Chess.com game viewer, ChessBase and the Guardian all give the same moves.)
