Left out on purpose, and why:
- CPR on the pitch, and by whom: accounts differ. Europa Press reported CPR on the grass; El País and the club put the
  return of his pulse in the ambulance. The court investigation (Policía Nacional homicide unit) is examining exactly
  this, along with the stadium's ambulance protocol, so the video shows only what is not in dispute: players calling
  for help, medics running on, the stretcher
  ([Atlántico Hoy](https://www.atlanticohoy.com/deportes-sa/investigacion-sobre-fallecimiento-jesus-miguel-pone-foco-en-atencion-medica-en-heliodoro_1569484_102.html),
  [Ara](https://es.ara.cat/deportes/futbol/no-entro-ambulancia-campo-tenerife_1_5902413.html));
- "commotio cordis": suggested by doctors in the press as the probable cause, but not confirmed; the video says
  "a preliminary autopsy points to the blow to his chest" and nothing more
  ([Ara](https://es.ara.cat/deportes/futbol/actuar-commotio-cordis-probable-causa-muerte-jesus-miguel_130_5900489.html));
- the investigation into possible involuntary manslaughter and the red card: the chief prosecutor said a criminal case
  against the goalkeeper was unlikely, as it was "una acción de juego recogida en el acta arbitral"; the video does
  not suggest any blame;
- his partner and daughter are not named.

Checked against the broadcast footage of the collision (LaLiga Hypermotion pictures, seen in news re-uploads on
9 November 2026, for reference only, nothing taken from them): the long clearance, de Miguel running onto it, Owono
leaping with his knee raised and his arms up; the keeper carried on past him, went down and sat on the grass, then got
up; de Miguel went straight to the ground. Andorra played in red, Owono in black with 25, the referee in yellow, the
medics wore orange; a sunny afternoon ([beIN Sports](https://www.beinsports.com/en-us/soccer/articles/could-jes%C3%BAs-owono-face-prison-time-over-jes%C3%BAs-de-miguel-s-death-2026-11-03-video_asi_fue_el_choque_de_jesus_de_miguel_con_ow)).

On screen, drawn rather than documented:
- every person is a cartoon, not a likeness; the faces are generic;
- the opening shows the blow and the X-ray first, before the story is told: the same reconstruction and the same
  illustration as in the main sequence, then the ball rolling to rest on its own;
- the run through the lower leagues is one generic, half-empty ground and a plain kit with no number or badge; the
  boards he runs past carry the names of his clubs in order as plain lettering (they are not real boards), with
  "MADRID · 2015" for his senior debut at Leganés B and "TENERIFE · 2025" for his move; the half-season loan at Unión
  Adarve is left off;
- the kits follow the footage (Tenerife white and blue; Andorra in their red away kit; Owono in black, 25; the
  referee in yellow; the medics in orange vests), drawn, not the exact 2026–27 designs; the advertising boards carry
  place names only;
- the paths of the ball and the players are a reconstruction from the footage and the descriptions above (the cross
  and the defender who clears it, shown close as he volleys it away, are drawn to set up the clearance the reports
  describe); who stood where round him afterwards, and each player's gesture (hands on the head, an arm waving the
  medics on), are drawn in general from the footage, not player by player;
- after the blow: AP says he first curled on his knees and then fell onto his back; once the X-ray has shown the heart
  stopping, the video shows him only going down onto his back and lying still, so that the picture never contradicts
  the cardiac arrest it has just shown;
- the X-ray is an illustration of the reported sequence (a knee to the chest, then cardiac arrest), not medical
  imaging: the bones, lungs and heart are drawn, the ribs give a little under the knee and spring back (no fracture
  is claimed), and the heart trace on it is schematic;
- the stretcher, the ambulance, the monitor in it (the pulse coming back: the club's statement; the moment the trace
  returns on screen is illustrative) and the outside of the ground (the wall, the name on it, the road) are drawn, not
  the real ones; where the ambulance waited during the match is not shown (it is part of the investigation);
- "Owono had run to him with the others" goes back to the pitch for a moment: the same reconstruction, slowed, with the
  keeper on his feet beside him, hands on his head (AP: he "turned around and joined the other players rushing to check
  on De Miguel");
- the hospital room, the clock coming round to eight and the monitor going flat illustrate "he died in hospital the
  next morning, at eight"; no patient is shown. The chest film on the light box with a ring round the chest
  illustrates "a preliminary autopsy points to the blow to his chest"; it is not the autopsy's imaging;
- the sunny afternoon follows the footage;
- the minute-9 tribute is drawn as one generic stadium standing for all of them.
