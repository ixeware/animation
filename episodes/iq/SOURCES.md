# The highest IQs in chess — sources

Almost every "IQ of a chess player" online is a rumour. The video says so on screen: only Kasparov's number comes
from a supervised test; the others are labelled "online test on stream", "the internet says", "reported".

| Claim in the video | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hikaru Nakamura scored 102 on an online IQ test, live on stream, while reading chat | casual free online test (around 2020–21), not a supervised IQ test | [Riot IQ — Hikaru Nakamura](https://www.riotiq.com/articles/famous-people-and-iq/what-is-hikaru-nakamura-s-iq) |
| Kasparov: 135, tested in 1987 for a German magazine (Der Spiegel); spatial skills below average | the only supervised test on the list (a battery assembled with H. J. Eysenck's team; 123 on Raven's matrices; spatial ability below the control group of German players). Reported in secondary sources; the December 1987 issue itself was not checked | [Riot IQ — Garry Kasparov](https://www.riotiq.com/articles/famous-people-and-iq/what-is-garry-kasparov-s-iq), [Lichess thread quoting the Spiegel piece](https://lichess.org/forum/general-chess-discussion/iq-score-of-the-high-chess-players?page=1) |
| Magnus Carlsen: "the internet says 190", he has never released a score | no published score; 190 is internet folklore | [Riot IQ — Magnus Carlsen](https://www.riotiq.com/articles/famous-people-and-iq/what-is-magnus-carlsen-s-iq) |
| Judit Polgar: reportedly 170 | unsourced popular figure, shown as "reported" | popular lists, e.g. [RD Asia](https://www.rdasia.com/?p=14334) |
| Polgar beat Kasparov: Moscow, 2002, rapid | Russia vs Rest of the World, 9 September 2002, rapid (25 min + 10 s); Kasparov had Black and resigned | [OlimpBase](https://olimpbase.org/2002g/2002in.html), [ChessBase](https://en.chessbase.com/post/what-is-wrong-with-the-great-ks-) |
| Fischer: "a school official put his IQ in the 180s" | anecdote in Frank Brady, *Profile of a Prodigy* (1973), p. 22, from an unnamed former member of Erasmus Hall's grade advisor's office. Other figures (184, Moran; 123, Pittsburgh Press 1972) are unverified too; the NYT wrote in 1958 that his IQ had never been made public | [Riot IQ — Bobby Fischer](https://www.riotiq.com/articles/famous-people-and-iq/what-was-bobby-fischer-s-iq) |
| Fischer left school at 16 (1959) and became world champion (1972) | well documented | Brady, *Endgame* (2011) |
| Einstein's estimated 160 | an estimate; Einstein never took an IQ test | — |
| Background: chess skill and intelligence | meta-analysis of 19 studies, r ≈ 0.24 | [Burgoyne et al. 2016 (MSU)](https://msutoday.msu.edu/news/2016/study-links-intelligence-and-chess-skill) |
