## Notes

- The TikTok's figures (2 November 2026): Messi 936 goals in 1,183 games, Ronaldo 985 in 1,343; match-winners 195 and
  243 (20.8% and 24.7%); wins by one goal in which they scored 111 and 143; points lost without their goals 362 and
  415. Its own count ("we checked all 1,921 of their goals"); I could not check the 111 and 143, so they are not used.
- What counts as a "match-winning goal" differs between counts (the goal that gave the lead for good in a win, two-leg
  ties by aggregate on messivsronaldo.app); the trackers' totals also move by a goal or two from week to week.
- Recorded and then cut for length (STYLE_BIBLE rule 70): "First, the basics" (the four totals, now on screen only) and
  "Wins that only came because they scored: Messi 177, Ronaldo 211" (michelacosta.com, wins generated with goals).
- Not in the film, worth knowing for the comments: 30 of Messi's 196 match-winners and 56 of Ronaldo's 240 were
  penalties (without them, 166 and 184); and counting assists as well as goals the points flip: Messi 578, Ronaldo 553
  (michelacosta.com, "Points won thanks to their goals and assists").
- After "So who stat-pads more?" the narration holds 2 s of silence before the verdict (the author's request for
  suspense, 12 November 2026); nothing was re-recorded.
- The narration is one ElevenLabs take of a longer script, cut at sentence ends (the month's quota was spent: no new
  take was possible); `script.json` says which sentences were cut.

## What is drawn, and what was not checked

- Kits: Barcelona in blue and garnet stripes with blue shorts and socks, Real Madrid all in white, Juventus in black and
  white stripes; no sponsors, a plain badge. Not the exact designs of any season.
- The matches of the hook, the match-winners, the 5–0 and Ronaldo's late winner are made up to show the claims (no
  opponent is named; the scores and minutes in the corner are part of the illustration, not records of real matches).
- The two replays are in black and white: what Real Madrid and Getafe wore those nights was not checked. In Turin the
  foot he struck it with, the cross and the corner of the net were not checked (drawn: the right foot, low to the
  goalkeeper's left); Messi's run is shortened and the defenders are generic figures.
- The "Siu" is Ronaldo's own celebration; drawn in a made-up match. The shout is not his voice: one ElevenLabs take of
  "SIUUU!" (the narrator's voice, Callum), layered with 14 lower and higher copies and a hall reverb into a stadium
  chant (`audio/samples/siu_0.flac`).
- The faces are cartoons, not likenesses: Messi today with his beard, Messi at 19 with long hair, Ronaldo clean-shaven.
