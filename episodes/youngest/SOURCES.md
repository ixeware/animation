# The youngest grandmasters ever — sources

| Claim in the video | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1958: Fischer becomes a grandmaster at fifteen, the youngest in history (plaque: 15 years 6 months 1 day; badge: Portorož 1958) | GM title through his result at the Portorož Interzonal (1958), at 15y 6m 1d, then the youngest ever | [Wikipedia: List of youngest grandmasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_youngest_grandmasters), [Wikipedia: Bobby Fischer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Fischer) |
| For thirty-three years no one could touch the record | Fischer 1958 → Polgár December 1991 | as above |
| 1991: Judit Polgár breaks it at fifteen years and four months; "a month younger than Fischer" | GM in December 1991 at 15y 4m 28d; 15y 6m 1d − 15y 4m 28d ≈ 1 month | [Wikipedia: List of youngest grandmasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_youngest_grandmasters), [Wikipedia: Judit Polgár](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judit_Polg%C3%A1r) |
| Then Leko, (Bacrot,) Ponomariov, Bu Xiangzhi — every few years someone younger | record progression: Leko 1994 (14y 4m 22d, Hungary), Bacrot 1997 (14y 2m 0d, France), Ponomariov 1997 (14y 0m 17d, Ukraine), Bu Xiangzhi 1999 (13y 10m 13d, China). Bacrot is shown on screen, not voiced | [Wikipedia: List of youngest grandmasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_youngest_grandmasters), [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/article/view/youngest-chess-grandmasters) |
| 2002: Sergey Karjakin, twelve years and seven months; the record stood for nineteen years | GM in August 2002 at 12y 7m 0d (then playing for Ukraine); broken by Mishra in June 2021 | [Wikipedia: List of youngest grandmasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_youngest_grandmasters) |
| 2019: an Indian boy missed it by seventeen days — Gukesh (New Delhi, January 2019) | GM on 15 January 2019 (Delhi International GM Open) at 12y 7m 17d, 17 days older than Karjakin had been | [Wikipedia: Gukesh Dommaraju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gukesh_Dommaraju), [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/players/gukesh-dommaraju), [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2019/01/worlds-second-youngest-grandmaster) |
| At eighteen he became world champion (Singapore, December 2024) | won the 2024 World Championship against Ding Liren in Singapore on 12 December 2024, aged 18 | [Wikipedia: World Chess Championship 2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Chess_Championship_2024) |
| 2021, Budapest: Abhimanyu Mishra, a kid from New Jersey, twelve years, four months, twenty-five days, the youngest grandmaster ever (map: New Jersey → Budapest) | third norm at the Vezérképző GM tournament in Budapest on 30 June 2021, at 12y 4m 25d; from New Jersey, USA | [US Chess](https://new.uschess.org/news/gm-abhimanyu-mishra-youngest-gm-history), [FIDE](https://www.fide.com/2021/06/30/), [ABC7](https://abc7news.com/post/youngest-grandmaster-12-year-old-chess-record-world-open-2021/10853751/) |
| This year Argentina's Faustino Oro fell just two months short | sealed the title in May 2026 (Sardinia World Chess Festival) at 12y 6m 26d, second-youngest ever; FIDE approved the title in August 2026. 12y 6m 26d − 12y 4m 25d = 2 months 1 day | [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/faustino-oro-seals-title-to-become-2nd-youngest-gm-ever-at-12), [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/faustino-oro-officially-becomes-worlds-youngest-grandmaster), [Wikipedia: Faustino Oro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faustino_Oro) |
| Roman Shogdzhiev, just eleven, already has two grandmaster norms (June 2026, Mongolia; October 2026, Serbia); +29 rating points to go | first norm at the Asian Individual Championship in Ulaanbaatar, June 2026, at 11y 4m 1d (the youngest GM norm ever); second norm at the Serbian Super League, October 2026; still needs a third norm and 29 rating points | [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/11-year-old-roman-shogdzhiev-becomes-youngest-ever-to-score-gm-norm), [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/roman-shogdzhiev-second-gm-norm-youngest-grandmaster-record), [Wikipedia: Roman Shogdzhiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Shogdzhiev) |
| If he gets the title before February, he'll be the first grandmaster under twelve (calendar: February 4, 2027, his 12th birthday) | he turns 12 on 4 February 2027; no one has completed the title before 12 (Mishra's 12y 4m 25d is the record) | [Chess.com](https://www.chess.com/news/view/roman-shogdzhiev-second-gm-norm-youngest-grandmaster-record) |
| The title: 3 norms + a 2500 rating | FIDE title regulations, as summarised | [Wikipedia: Grandmaster (chess)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grandmaster_(chess)) |
