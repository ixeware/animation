## Drawn, not documented

- The Baku stage is redrawn after the broadcast frame (blue table, wooden board, digital clock, a dark blue wall; no
  sponsor logos); the players are cartoon likenesses. Hikaru's walk and grimace follow the footage.
- The memory test on our board (five seconds, the pieces drive away and come back, then scramble) illustrates the
  experiments; it is not a real experiment with Hikaru.
- The blindfold game: there is no public record of the whole game in our sources, so the "board in his head" over the
  Barcelona stage shows only the move the reports name, 26...Bxe3!!, on an otherwise empty board.
- The streaming room is generic, not Hikaru's real studio; the 34 win tiles stand for the 34 wins. His facecam (the
  room seen from his monitor), the captcha and the teal power-up are jokes on the narration, not events.
- The sea with the Stockfish fish is a picture of the pun (as in the author's Mittens video), not a claim about the
  engine.
