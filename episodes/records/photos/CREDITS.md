# Facts and sources

- #5 Fastest time to arrange a chess set: 29.85 s, one hand, Puducherry, India, 20 July 2021 (Guinness World Records, "Fastest time to arrange a chess set"). The record holder appears to be a minor, so the name is not shown.
- #4 Largest chess piece: a king 6.09 m (20 ft) tall, 4,926 kg (10,860 lb), World Chess Hall of Fame, Saint Louis, 6 April 2018 (Guinness World Records "Largest chess piece"; worldchesshof.org "World's Largest Chess Piece").
- #3 GM Ehsan Ghaem-Maghami, Tehran, February 2011: 604 simultaneous games over 25+ hours, 580 wins, 16 draws, 8 losses. Guinness counts 135 of the games, because only opponents rated 1200+ qualify (Wikipedia, "Ehsan Ghaem Maghami"; Guinness World Records "Most simultaneous games of chess by an individual").
- #2 Longest chess marathon: 64 hours, Tunde Onakoya and Shawn Martinez, Times Square, New York, 17–20 April 2025, 473 games (Guinness World Records "Longest chess marathon"; Chess.com news).
- #1 GM Timur Gareyev: 48 simultaneous blindfold games, Las Vegas, 3–4 December 2016, +35 =7 −6, riding an exercise bike for much of the event (Chess.com news "Timur Gareyev plays blindfold on 48 boards"; recognized by Guinness).
