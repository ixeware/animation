Stockfish, the strongest chess "fish", only surfaced in the year he died. The metaphor is ours; every number and date
in the video is checked below.

## Drawn, not documented

- The sea, the boat, the fishing rod and the fish: our metaphor. Stockfish was not "waiting"; the two dates are real.
- Fischer, Taimanov, Larsen, Spassky and Carlsen appear as cartoon doubles.
- The Stockfish fish is a cut-out from the Stockfish logo (Klein Maetschke, GPLv3), as in `hikaru213`; credit it in
  the frame.

## Not to say

- That Fischer would beat Stockfish (3650 against 2785).
- "Best player of all time by rating": Carlsen's rating is higher; Fischer's record is the lead over No. 2.
- Taimanov's "at least I still have my music": no source found.
- Fischer's politics.
