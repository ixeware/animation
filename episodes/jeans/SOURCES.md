# Magnus Quit Because of Jeans — facts and sources

- FIDE World Rapid & Blitz Championships, New York, December 2024. On the second day of the rapid (27 December) Magnus Carlsen arrived in jeans, which the event's dress code prohibits. The chief arbiter fined him $200 and asked him to change; he said he would change the next day. FIDE did not pair him for round 9, and he withdrew from both the rapid and the blitz. Ian Nepomniachtchi had been fined the same day for sports shoes, changed and kept playing (AP / NBC News; CNN; FIDE statement).
- Two days later FIDE president Arkady Dvorkovich said officials could allow "appropriate jeans" with a jacket and other "elegant minor deviations" from the dress code; Carlsen returned for the blitz, wearing jeans (AP; CNN).
- World Blitz final, 31 December 2024: Carlsen led Nepomniachtchi 2–0, Nepomniachtchi levelled, then three sudden-death games were drawn; Carlsen proposed sharing the title and FIDE agreed. Chess.com calls it the first individual world championship title ever shared (Chess.com; CNN).
- The jeans were auctioned on eBay for charity and sold for $36,100 in March 2025; proceeds went to Big Brothers Big Sisters of America (AP; JustCollecting).
